# Comparable Website Survey: Travel Planning and Group Expenses

**Reviewed**: 2026-11-02  
**Method**: Public official product pages only; no account creation or
authenticated workflows were inspected. Findings below summarize claims visible
on the cited pages and are not independent product tests.

## Findings

| Website | Publicly described capabilities | Relevance to Cùng Đi | Scope boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/) | The homepage presents detailed trip itineraries, an itinerary and map in one view, user-shared guides, and booking management. | Relevant to FR-006's trip itinerary and destination planning. The combined itinerary/map presentation is a reference for considering how a plan's activities and destination can be navigated together. | Do not infer that Cùng Đi needs public guides, bookings, maps, or any particular layout; those are not approved requirements. |
| [TripIt](https://www.tripit.com/web) | The homepage describes forwarding booking confirmation emails to create an itinerary, plus flight alerts and travel reminders. | Relevant as an example of consolidating travel details into an itinerary, which can inform the clarity of Plan/itinerary information in FR-006. | Email-imported bookings, flight monitoring, and travel alerts are not part of the confirmed Cùng Đi scope. Project email is limited to the events in FR-014. |
| [Splitwise](https://www.splitwise.com/) | The product page lists groups and friends, equal or unequal expense splits, totals/balances, expense categories and search, charts, debt simplification, and payment integrations. | Relevant to FR-008/FR-009's group-expense allocation and computed totals. It supports keeping expense entry, allocation, and summary concepts distinct in the interface and tests. | Cùng Đi records VND expenses and member shares/differences only. Debt settlement, repayment, payment integration, and real-money transactions are explicitly out of scope. |

## Product implications

1. Keep each Plan's itinerary and its single destination easy to locate and
   understand; this is a design reference, not approval to add a map or public
   guide feature.
2. Keep expense capture and allocation tied to a Plan, and calculate summaries
   from the approved expense/member data. Do not copy competitors' debt,
   settlement, or payment behavior.
3. Use competitor pages to inform comparison and design discussion only. They
   do not establish Cùng Đi requirements, permissions, integrations, or UI
   layouts; existing approved requirements remain authoritative.
4. This short public-page survey does not assess registration flows, responsive
   behavior, accessibility, pricing, or authenticated product screens.
